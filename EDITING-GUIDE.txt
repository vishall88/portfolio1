SACHCHIDANAND GUPT — PORTFOLIO V3

OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP completely.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Keep the assets and videos folders beside index.html.

EDIT PHOTOS / POSTERS
- Replace files in assets/ (keep the same filename), or edit the matching src="assets/..." in index.html.
- hero-person-enhanced.png is the gently enhanced portrait used on the front page.
- hero-person-original.png is your original transparent cutout backup.

EDIT VIDEOS
- Replace the files in videos/ while keeping these names:
  krishna-color-grade.mp4
  color-grade-reel.mp4
  day-to-night.mp4
- To add a video card, copy an existing <article class="video-card"> block in index.html and update its video path and title.

EDIT TEXT / CONTACT
- Open index.html in VS Code, Notepad++, or any text editor.
- Search for text you want to change. Contact links are in the Contact section.

NOTES
- Internet is only needed for the Google Fonts import; the layout, images and videos are local.
- The site adapts to desktop and mobile. Animations respect the device's reduced-motion setting.
